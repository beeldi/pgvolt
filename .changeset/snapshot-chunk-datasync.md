"@core/sync-service": patch
---

A shape's snapshot chunks, each synced and closed through the file handle that wrote it, the record of the snapshot's end and every directory entry leading to them, up through the `shapes` entry in `ELECTRIC_STORAGE_DIR`, are now durable before the shape records its snapshot as complete; that entry is left to the host when the service may not open `ELECTRIC_STORAGE_DIR` and either may not write it or `shapes` is mounted there from another filesystem. As the stack starts it also syncs the entry naming `ELECTRIC_STORAGE_DIR` and each one above it that the service could have created (in a directory it may write, on the same device as the one the entry names), up to the first directory it cannot open, which it leaves to the host and logs; it syncs none of them when `shapes` or `ELECTRIC_STORAGE_DIR` is mounted from another filesystem. Before, a machine crash or a write error reported late could keep the snapshot marked complete while losing the record or a chunk's bytes, and readers then served what the disk returned in their place. A reader waiting for a snapshot's last chunk also no longer skips it when the writer creates the chunk and records the end while it waits, and a read that starts as the end is being recorded no longer skips the log entries past it.
